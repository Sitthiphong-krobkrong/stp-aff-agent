# Media Rights & Publishing Compliance

This is operational guidance, not legal advice. Validate current platform terms and licensing rules when implementing.

## Rights-first source preference
1. Original footage/photos taken by the operator or contracted creators with explicit required rights.
2. Product/brand media supplied under documented written commercial license valid on both target platforms.
3. Third-party stock images/videos with compatible commercial use, modification, branding and distribution permissions, after validating specific asset terms.
4. AI-generated supplemental visuals only under model terms permitting commercial use, and never to fake unsupported product functions or actual testing.

## Never assume rights automatically
- Shopee listing images, brand logos, seller demonstration videos, customer reviews, TikTok clips, copyrighted music or voice samples.
- A stock library's general commercial license resolves every trademark, privacy, appearance or third-party-rights issue.
- TikTok commercial music rights automatically cover reupload to Shopee.
- Open-source TTS client library license automatically grants commercial rights to hosted synthesized voice.

## Rights gate (fail closed)
For each embedded asset, capture source URL/owner, download date, license name/URL or permission evidence, exact allowed uses and platforms, attribution requirements, expiration, SHA-256, reviewer and approval status. Reject candidate or choose another if uncertainty persists. Do not mark `verified` merely on LLM inference.

## Claims gate
- Product claims must be traceable to reliable source evidence.
- Do not say 'tested', 'I used', battery capacity confirmed, warranty guaranteed, or sales proven without actual evidence.
- AI motion/illustration must not be presented as actual product operation.
- Note commercial/affiliate sponsorship in captions/comments according to platform rules.
- Determine if platform requires AI-generated content disclosure, especially for realistic synthetic footage.

## Video master
Neutral 9:16 MP4; no Shopee/TikTok watermark or platform-exclusive music. Keep Shopee captions, TikTok captions and Affiliate Unique Code comment separately. Only use a code that maps to the specific verified seller listing; do not universalize historical sample codes.

## Reviewer checklist
- Product identity/variant verified
- All assets licensed for commercial reuse on Shopee & TikTok
- Thai audio usage rights clear
- Thai font commercial license clear
- Music covers both platforms or omitted
- Content claims substantiated
- AI-content and affiliate disclosures assessed
- Publishing authorized and only after approval
